About the Studio

A philosophy of wellbeing, shaped in space

Twasthi believes that every space should tell a meaningful story while enhancing the way people live. At its core, Twasthi Design Studioz is committed to designing spaces that are not driven by trends, but by timeless principles of beauty, authenticity, and craftsmanship—transforming dreams into thoughtfully crafted realities.

Our process begins with listening: to how a family moves through their day, to the materials a region has perfected over centuries, and to the light a room already holds. From there, architecture and interior design are considered as one discipline, not two — so that every wall, joinery detail and finish belongs to a single, unified idea.
