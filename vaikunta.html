Vaikunt

LocationHyderabad
ScopeFull Interior Design

The Story

A home built around heritage and light

At Vaikunt, every space is a quiet celebration of India's timeless craftsmanship. Rooted in tradition yet designed for contemporary living, the home seamlessly weaves together handcrafted woodwork, curated heirloom pieces, natural materials, and artisanal details to create interiors that feel warm, authentic, and enduring. From the welcoming entrance to the thoughtfully detailed bedrooms, living spaces, kitchen, and home office, every corner reflects a deep respect for heritage while embracing modern functionality. Designed and executed by Twasthi Design Studioz, this residence is a testament to our philosophy that meaningful design is never about excess—it is about creating homes with soul, where every material, texture, and handcrafted element tells a story. Vaikunt is not merely a home; it is a timeless expression of culture, comfort, and craftsmanship, sculpted to be cherished for generations.

Design Concept

Carved heritage, quiet luxury

Vaikunt is a contemporary Indian home that celebrates heritage through thoughtful, timeless design. Inspired by traditional architecture and artisanal craftsmanship, the interiors seamlessly blend handcrafted elements with modern functionality. Rich teak wood, exposed brick, brass accents, natural stone, and curated Indian art create a warm, authentic palette that feels both elegant and enduring. Every space has been designed to tell a story—where carved furniture, handcrafted décor, and indigenous materials become an integral part of everyday living rather than mere embellishments. Natural light, earthy textures, and a restrained colour palette enhance the home's sense of calm, comfort, and belonging. The design reflects a philosophy of preserving cultural identity while embracing contemporary lifestyles. Rather than following fleeting trends, Vaikunt focuses on creating meaningful spaces that age gracefully and remain relevant for generations. The result is a home that is rooted in tradition, refined in expression, and thoughtfully crafted to embody warmth, functionality, and timeless Indian luxury.
