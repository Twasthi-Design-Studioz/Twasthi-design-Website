Villa Interiors

Madhuram

LocationHyderabad
ScopeFull Interior Design

The Story

A house that feels "Home"

Warm wood tones, earthy neutrals, handcrafted textiles, and sculptural lighting establish a cohesive visual language throughout the home. Bespoke furniture pieces and carefully selected décor add character, while traditional Indian artworks and intricate detailing bring a strong sense of identity to each space.

Madhuram is ultimately a celebration of Indian craftsmanship interpreted through a modern lens—a home that feels personal, warm, timeless, and sophisticated. It reflects Twasthi Design Studioz’s approach to creating interiors that are not simply visually beautiful, but also meaningful, experiential, and connected to the people who inhabit them.

Design Concept

Mordern living in Indian style

The design focuses on warmth, comfort, and cultural identity, with each space balancing functionality and visual character. Bespoke furniture, layered lighting, sculptural details, and curated artworks add depth while maintaining a cohesive, timeless aesthetic. The result is a home that feels rooted, personal, and sophisticated—celebrating Indian design traditions in a contemporary, understated manner.

Materials Used
Hand-carved Teak Italian Marble Tandur stone flooring Micro Cementing Handloom Textiles
